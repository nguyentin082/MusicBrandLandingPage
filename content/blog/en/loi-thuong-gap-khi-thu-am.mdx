Even experienced people can still make mistakes when recording. Understanding **common mistakes when recording** will save you a lot of time editing post-production.

## 1. 12 most common mistakes

1. Too high gain causes clipping.
2. Background noise from fans/air conditioners.
3. Pop due to not using pop filter.
4. Sibilance is too strong in the sounds “s”, “sh”.
5. Missing rhythm at the end of the sentence.
6. Shortness of breath in the middle of long sentences.
7. Uneven volume of takes.
8. Misplaced mic.
9. Too much echo chamber.
10. Breathe clearly into the mic.
11. Do not backup raw files.
12. Installing the plugin too early without cleaning it up.

## 2. Error table and quick solutions

| Error | How to handle |
| --------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Clips | Reduce input gain, retract if distortion is severe |
| Background Noise | Turn off the noise source, lightly gate after recording |
| Sibilance | Place the mic off-axis, use de-esser |
| Room bounce | Add sound absorption, get closer to the mic |

## 3. 3-step rule before each session

- **Check room:** reduce noise and reflections.
- **Check mic:** test distance and singing angle.
- **Check signal:** ensures safe level.

## 4. FAQ

### After recording, I discovered whether the clip could be saved?

If the clip is light, the sensation can be reduced by processing, but most should still be retracted.

### Do you need a professional studio to produce clean vocals?

Optional. You can still achieve good results with the right setup and disciplined workflow.
