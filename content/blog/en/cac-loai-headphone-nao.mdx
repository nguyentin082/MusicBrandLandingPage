In music production, the question "**what types of headphones are there**" is very important because each type of headphone serves a different goal: recording, editing or mixing.

## 1. Closed-back headphones (closed headphones)

- Less sound leaks out, suitable for recording vocals.
- Bass is often felt more clearly.
- Wearing it for a long time can be more uncomfortable than open-back.

## 2. Open-back headphones (open headphones)

- Airy soundstage, easy to hear spatial details.
- Suitable for mixing/reviewing mixes.
- Not suitable for recording because sound easily enters the mic.

## 3. In-ear monitor (IEM)

- Compact, easy to carry.
- Personal performance and monitoring.
- Need good ear fit to hear the correct frequency range.

## 4. Quick menu by purpose

| Purpose | Recommended type |
| --------------- | ------------- |
| Vocal recording | Closed-back |
| Mixing details | Open-back |
| Move a lot | IEM |

## 5. 5 criteria for choosing headphones for producers

1. Long-term wearing comfort.
2. The frequency response is relatively flat.
3. Durability and replacement parts.
4. Impedance matching audio interface.
5. Ability to reproduce mid (vocal area).

## 6. FAQ

### Can I mix completely with headphones?

It is possible, but you should cross-check with speakers and common listening devices.

### Are expensive headphones required?

Optional. It is important to understand the characteristics of the device you are using.
