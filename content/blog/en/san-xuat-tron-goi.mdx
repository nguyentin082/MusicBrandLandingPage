## Arrangement: Breathing Life into Your Melody

Arrangement is the process of dressing up your melody. This is when instruments, drums, bass, and backing vocal layers are arranged harmoniously.

A good arrangement highlights your vocal tone and brings out the song message. We experiment with different sound colors alongside you to find the ideal version before stepping into the recording studio.

## Recording: Capturing Genuine Emotion

Once the arrangement is ready, the next step is recording at the recording studio. Before you step up to the microphone, 2lab engineers guide you through vocal warm-ups, breathing techniques, and help you get into a relaxed mindset.

Throughout the session, we stay right there with you to capture every peak of genuine emotion. A clean, well-timed vocal take free of background noise serves as a solid foundation for the next stages of sound processing.

## Editing, Mixing, and Mastering: Polishing Sound Quality

After capturing the raw audio tracks, our engineers handle detailed pitch and timing correction for every single phrase so your voice sounds natural yet polished.

Next comes mixing, where we balance the volume between vocals and instruments, creating a three-dimensional space for your track. Finally, mastering fine-tunes the overall volume, brightness, and depth so the track reaches professional standards and is ready for release across all platforms.
