If you're new to audio, the biggest question is often **what is a microphone and how many types are there**. A mic is a device that converts sound vibrations into electrical signals for recording in a DAW.

## 1. The 3 most popular types of microphones

### Dynamic microphone

- Durable, withstands loud sounds well.
- Live music and rooms that have not been thoroughly acoustically treated.

### Condenser microphone

- High sensitivity, good detail capture.
- Needs 48V phantom power.
- Suitable for vocal studio recording.

### Ribbon microphone

- Soft, natural, vintage sound.
- Sensitive and needs appropriate environment/equipment.

## 2. Quick comparison

| Microphone type | Advantages                    | Limitations                    |
| --------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Dynamic         | Durable, low noise            | Less detailed than condenser   |
| Condenser       | Detailed, bright, wide range  | Easy to detect room noise      |
| Ribbons         | Beautiful, smooth sound color | Higher price/conditions of use |

## 3. Choose the mic according to your needs

- Recording vocals at home without good soundproofing: prioritize dynamic.
- Record detailed vocals for pop/ballad: condenser.
- Collect woodwind instruments with soft tones: ribbon (if possible).

## 4. Basic mic placement tips

1. Use a pop filter 5-10 cm away from the mic.
2. Mouth 12-20 cm away from the mic depending on the voice.
3. Sing slightly off-axis to reduce aspirated sounds.

## 5. FAQ

### Do expensive mics always record better?

Are not. Quality also depends on the studio, preamp, singing technique and mic placement.

### Which one should a newbie buy first?

It's usually a good idea to start with a mid-range or versatile dynamic condenser, depending on the room environment.
