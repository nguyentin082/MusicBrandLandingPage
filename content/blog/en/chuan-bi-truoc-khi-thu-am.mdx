A good recording starts with **pre-recording preparation**. When set up correctly, you significantly reduce noise, out-of-tune, and errors that have to be manually corrected later.

## 1. Technical checklist before pressing REC

1. Check gain input, avoid clipping.
2. Turn off the fan/noise source near the mic.
3. Use pop filter and shock mount.
4. Choose a sample rate/bit depth consistent with the project.
5. Create a track, name it clearly.
6. Turn on the metronome or reference beat at the correct tempo.

## 2. Checklist for singers

- Warm up your voice for 10-15 minutes.
- Drink warm water and limit cold foods before autumn.
- Mark the breathing space in the lyric.
- Practice 1-2 times to find the right energy.

## 3. Checklist workflow to save time

| Category | How to optimize |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Compiling | Collect 3-5 quality takes instead of 15 spread out takes |
| Backups | Save the project and copy the raw file according to the timestamp |
| Notes | Note the error sentence immediately, avoid listening to the whole song again |

## 4. Mistakes make the autumn session last longer

- Record without warming up your voice.
- Do not test the headphone mix first.
- Move the mic distance continuously.
- Inconsistent key and tempo of the mix.

## 5. FAQ

### Should I record dry or add effects when the singer listens?

You should record dry, but you can let the singer listen to light reverb/delay in his ears to ease the emotions.

### How many takes per verse?

Usually 3-5 good takes are enough for effective comping.
