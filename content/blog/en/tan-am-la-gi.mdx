When talking about voice quality, many producers mention harmonics. So what is sound frequency? These are sound components that appear with the root note, helping the voice sound thick, bright and have its own color.

## 1. How does sound frequency work?

Every time you sing a note, your ears don't just hear a single frequency. It also listens to many accompanying sub-frequencies in natural proportions. This collection creates the "voice" of each person.

## 2. Why is sound frequency important when recording vocals?

- Helps vocals cut through the background music better.
- Creates a warm or bright feeling depending on the resonance area.
- Is the foundation for effective EQ and saturation.

## 3. How to safely increase useful sound frequencies

### Before recording

- Warm up lightly with hum and lip trill.
- Drink warm water to keep your vocal cords flexible.
- Choose a tone that matches your comfort level.

### While recording

- Keep the mic distance stable.
- Sing clearly the consonants and open the vowels just enough.
- Avoid squeezing the throat to "push the light".

### After recording

- Use EQ to cut away the turbidity before increasing the brightness.
- Added slight saturation to complement harmonics.

## 4. Summary table of common sensory areas

| Frequency range | Common feelings |
| ----------- | ---------------------- |
| 100-250 Hz | Warm, thick, prone to buzzing |
| 2-5 kHz | Clear text, presence |
| 8-12 kHz | Airiness, brightness, “air” |

## 5. FAQ

### Is the audio frequency the base frequency?

Are not. The base frequency is the main note; Sound frequencies are accompanying components.

### Is it possible to “generate” audio frequencies entirely with a plugin?

Plugin support is good, but the quality of the recording source and singing technique are still the deciding factors.
