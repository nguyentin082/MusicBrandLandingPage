## What Is Vocal Harmony and Why Your Song Needs It

Vocal harmony is the art of combining different melodies or notes that blend with the main vocal melody to create a richer overall sound. When you listen to a song and the vocals sound thicker, warmer, and have more depth than usual, that is thanks to the support of harmony parts.

During recording at a recording studio, adding harmonies keeps the lead vocal from sounding isolated while filling gaps in the arrangement (hòa âm phối khí). Harmonies act like secondary and tertiary layers of color that bring a musical canvas to life.

## Exploring Thirds in Vocal Harmony

Thirds (quãng 3) refer to the interval between two notes spaced three scale steps apart. This is the most popular choice when you want to harmonize with a lead vocal because this distance creates a pleasant harmony for the listener's ear, neither too wide nor too narrow to make an impact.

When singing third harmonies, singers need to practice listening to the main melody so their notes do not go out of tune and instead blend tightly with the lead vocal. This combination delivers a sweet feel and appears frequently in modern pop songs.

## Giving Weight to Your Melody with Fifths

Fifths (quãng 5) span five steps on the musical scale, bringing a completely different tonal color compared to thirds. The sound of a fifth typically carries resonance, stability, and spatial extension for a song.

Fifths fit well during climactic sections when a song needs an explosion of energy or a strong emotional anchor. When applied correctly, fifths make the overall vocal presence grander and more majestic.

## The Structure of 4-Part Choir Arrangements

In traditional music and choir settings, the standard 4-part choir (4 bè trong hợp xướng) structure consists of Soprano, Alto, Tenor, and Bass as the core foundation. Soprano and Alto usually cover higher female vocal ranges, while Tenor and Bass handle the warmer, lower male ranges.

Each part takes on a specific frequency range and unique task. When all four parts sing together, they form a thick block of sound with immense spatial depth, delivering powerful emotions to listeners.

## Applying Harmonies in Song Production

To achieve great harmony parts, the first step is to brainstorm harmony ideas that match the mood of the arrangement (hòa âm phối khí). Harmonies should not sound cluttered if the instrumental backing is already busy, and vice versa.

Before stepping into the recording studio for recording (thu âm), you should practice each harmony part separately until smooth so that the vocalists can blend seamlessly. Later, during the mixing (mix) and mastering (master) stages, the engineer balances levels and spatial depth so the harmonies support the lead vocal perfectly.
